️**Hello everyone, my name is Taufik Iskandar. I am currently working as a Data Analyst with over one year of experience. I graduated with a degree in Computer Science from Universitas Amikom Yogyakarta.

After graduation, I worked both as a freelancer and full-time at various companies. With over a year of experience as a Data Analyst, I have a strong focus on data management and visualization. I am experienced in handling large datasets (over 100,000 records), cleaning data, and performing data analysis to generate insights that support strategic decision-making.

I also have experience in creating interactive dashboards, monitoring customer retention, and identifying participation trends. I am proficient in Python, SQL, Excel, and Looker Studio, and I excel at presenting analytical findings clearly to stakeholders..
